Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be an advanced tennis player?
No. The training is designed for recreational tennis enthusiasts. You'll share your experience and playing level during registration so we can plan appropriate groups. Final coaching groups are subject to confirmation.
Can I travel alone?
Absolutely! Register for single occupancy, or share accommodations with another player. Shared-room arrangements must be confirmed before booking.
Can I bring someone who doesn't play tennis?
Yes! Non-playing companions are welcome and receive a $300 discount compared with the shared-occupancy tennis player rate.
Is airfare included?
No. Guests arrange and purchase their own airfare to Cancún International Airport (CUN).
Are airport transfers included?
Yes. Round-trip airport-to-resort transportation is included, subject to the final transfer arrangements communicated before departure.
Is the resort all-inclusive?
Yes! TRS Coral Hotel is a five-star, adults-only, all-inclusive resort with a wide variety of dining, drinks, activities, and entertainment. Certain specialty services and experiences carry additional charges.
Is the massage included?
Yes. Every registered guest, players and companions alike, receives one 50-minute sports or relaxation massage. You can book it on Saturday or Sunday.
Is afternoon tennis mandatory?
No. Afternoon match play is completely optional.
Will there be time to enjoy the resort?
Absolutely. The getaway is designed to balance tennis with relaxation, social activities, and free time.
Is CHIC Cabaret included?
TRS Coral guests receive complimentary admission, but a separate reservation fee applies. CHIC is an optional experience, not a prepaid Club Osa group activity.
Is travel insurance included?
No. We recommend purchasing travel insurance appropriate for your circumstances, including trip cancellation and medical coverage.
How does Early Bird pricing work?
The first 6 people to sign up receive Early Bird pricing, which saves $200 per person. After that, regular pricing applies.
What is the deposit and cancellation policy?
Deposit amount, payment schedule, and cancellation terms will be provided before registration is finalized.
